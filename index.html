Day 1 Theme

Downtown Gourmet Crawl & Smokehouse Comfort

Nov 12
9:00 AM – 10:30 AM

Artisanal Breakfast at Common Good Bakery

Downtown TC

Start with freshly milled Michigan grain pastries, whipped ricotta toast with blistered heirloom tomatoes, croque madames, and handcrafted espresso beverages.

11:00 AM – 3:00 PM • INDOOR CULINARY CRAWL

Downtown TC Culinary Tasting Crawl

Front Street Covered Shops

Stroll Front Street's covered brick storefronts for a cozy, weather-protected foodie afternoon:

🫒 Fustini's Oils & Vinegars: Sample artisanal extra virgin olive oils and 18-year aged balsamic vinegars at their tasting bar.
🍒 American Spoon: Taste small-batch Michigan fruit preserves, cherry butter, and gourmet condiments.
🍒 Cherry Republic: Sample cherry wines, hard ciders, cherry salsas, and gourmet dark chocolate cherries at their tasting counter.
4:30 PM – 7:00 PM • CULINARY HIGHLIGHT

Dinner & Drinks at Oaky's Tavern (Grawn)

Walk-Ins Welcome

Experience a cozy local smokehouse staple in Grawn. Oaky's is famous for daily in-house slow-smoked meats, friendly neighborhood pub atmosphere, and excellent draft beers.

🔥 Dish Recommendations at Oaky's Tavern:
  • Famous Jumbo Wings: Smoked daily, tossed in Dry Rub, Honey Jalapeño, or Chipotle BBQ.
  • Smokehouse Mac: Cavatappi in house cheese sauce, topped with smoked pulled pork & haystack onions.
  • Thai Pork Tacos: Smoked pork, sweet chili sauce, cabbage, and Thai mustard.
  • Smokehouse Burger: Half-pound fresh patty with smoked bacon and cheddar.
Day 2 Theme

Old Mission Peninsula Wine Trail & Waterfront Fine Dining

Nov 13
9:30 AM – 11:00 AM

Brunch at Red Spire Brunch House

GT Commons

Located inside The Village at Grand Traverse Commons. Stuffed French toast, savory omelets, and eggs Benedict with locally sourced ingredients.

11:30 AM – 1:30 PM • ANCHOR WINERY VISIT

Brys Estate Vineyard & Winery

Seated Tasting

155 contiguous acres of estate vineyards. In November, guests enjoy seated indoor tastings in the brick-and-mahogany room with curated Wine Tasting Towers.

🍷 Featured Tasting Towers: Reserve Cabernet Franc, Estate Gewürztraminer, Signature Pinot Noir, Dry Riesling.
🧀 Food Pairings: Artisanal charcuterie boards, warm baguettes, house hummus, gourmet wine sandwiches.
1:45 PM – 3:30 PM

Bowers Harbor Vineyards & Chateau Chantal

Mid-Peninsula

Stop first at unpretentious Bowers Harbor for crisp Pinot Grigio, single-vineyard Riesling, or hard ciders. Continue to Chateau Chantal, perched on a high ridge offering panoramic views of both East and West Grand Traverse Bays while sipping Michigan ice wines and estate white blends.

3:45 PM – 5:15 PM

Mari Vineyards & Underground Cave Tour

Italianate Estate

Italianate stone facility featuring subterranean barrel caves. Enjoy a tour and seated flight of signature estate wines paired with a curated cheese board.

6:15 PM – 8:30 PM • WATERFRONT FINE DINING

The Boathouse Restaurant (Bowers Harbor)

Reservations Recommended

Perched right on West Grand Traverse Bay in Bowers Harbor. Cottage-style nautical elegance, picturesque sunset views, and a refined menu highlighting fresh Great Lakes seafood and prime steaks.

🍽️ Must-Try Dishes: Pan-seared Great Lakes whitefish, Australian rack of lamb, oysters on the half shell, prime Filet Mignon, extensive regional & international wine cellar.
Day 3 Theme

Leelanau Wine & Craft Beer Trail to Premier 16th-Floor Steakhouse

Nov 14
10:00 AM – 11:30 AM

Morning Coffee & Downtown Front Street Stroll

Outpost / The Dandy

Enjoy coffee and pastries at Outpost Traverse City or The Dandy, followed by a boutique walk visiting independent local wine merchants and galleries.

12:00 PM – 1:30 PM • CULINARY HIGHLIGHT

Farm Club (Leelanau County)

First-Come Seating

A sleek Scandinavian farm-restaurant-brewery hybrid. Over 90% of produce is grown on-site. Warm up by outdoor fire pits with wild-fermentation ales.

🥗 Menu Highlights: Stone-milled grain pastas, hearth-baked sourdough, house-fermented pickles, estate farmhouse ciders and ales.
1:45 PM – 3:30 PM

MAWBY Sparkling Wines & Shady Lane Cellars

Leelanau Trail

Head to MAWBY for traditional method sparkling wine flights paired with savory bites. Then visit Shady Lane Cellars for 100-percent estate-grown, SIP-certified sustainable Pinot Noir, Cabernet Franc, and Dry Riesling in a rustic stone building.

3:45 PM – 5:15 PM • FIRESIDE CRAFT BEER STOP

Hop Lot Brewing Co. (Suttons Bay)

First-Come Beer Garden & Igloos

Located in Suttons Bay nestled in a soaring pine grove. Features open roaring outdoor fire pits, cozy heated igloos, and handcrafted small-batch beers.

🍺 Draft & Beverage Highlights: Highway Robbery IPA, Uncle Percy's Amber, Heavy Hoppin' Double IPA, hot mulled cider.
🔥 Vibe & Food: Sit by open campfires with a campfire s'mores kit, warm soft pretzel with beer cheese, or smoked pulled pork.
6:30 PM – 8:30 PM • FAREWELL ULTRA-UPSCALE STEAKHOUSE DINNER

Aerie Restaurant & Lounge (16th Floor Tower)

Reservations Recommended

Northern Michigan's premier high-end steakhouse fine dining experience. Located on the 16th floor of the Grand Traverse Resort tower, offering unmatched 360-degree panoramic glass views across East Grand Traverse Bay and rolling peninsula vineyards.

🥩 Prime Steaks & Chops: Dry-Aged USDA Prime Delmonico Ribeye, Center-Cut Prime Filet Mignon, Wagyu Strip, Roasted Prime Rib, Elk Loin.
🍷 Seafood & Cellar: Cold water lobster tail, seared sea scallops, truffle potato puree, tableside craft cocktails, Sommelier-curated international wine list.

Planning, Logistics & Seasonal Tips

Mid-November is Traverse City's cozy shoulder season. Use this operational guide and interactive checklist to keep your plans organized.

🌇 Daylight & Operating Hours

Plan for 5:15 PM Sunsets

November days are short. Schedule outdoor beer garden visits like Hop Lot between 11:00 AM and 4:30 PM to enjoy daylight by the fire pits.

🚘 Vehicle & Driving Strategy

Rural Peninsula Driving

An AWD vehicle is recommended for rural routes along M-37, M-22, and US-31, as sudden autumn lake-effect snow showers can occur in mid-November.

🧥 Warm Outdoor Attire

Campfire Layering

While indoor tasting rooms and Front Street shops are cozy, outdoor campfires at Hop Lot Brewing Co. and Farm Club require warm coats and boots.
