Curated Autumn Escape

Traverse City Getaway

This trip is structured for maximum comfort and geographic efficiency: exploring Downtown Front Street's culinary tasting crawl and handcrafted Italian pasta at Flying Noodle on Thursday, the iconic Old Mission Peninsula wine trail anchored by Brys Estate, Bonobo Winery, and waterfront dining at The Boathouse Restaurant on Friday, and a sit-down lunch at Oaky's Tavern, Leelanau Peninsula wine & beer, and ultra-upscale 16th-floor steakhouse dining at Aerie Restaurant & Lounge on Saturday.

Geographic Focus Areas

Thursday

Downtown TC Corridor

Breakfast at Common Good Bakery, artisanal food crawl along Front Street, and hand-extruded fresh pasta at Flying Noodle.

Friday

Old Mission Peninsula

M-37 wine trail featuring Brys Estate towers, Bowers Harbor, Chateau Chantal, Bonobo Winery, Mari cave tour, and Boathouse dinner.

Saturday

Grawn, Leelanau & Acme Tower

Lunch at Oaky's Tavern, MAWBY sparkling wines, Shady Lane Cellars, Hop Lot outdoor campfires, and 16th-floor dinner at Aerie.

Thursday Focus: Downtown Gourmet Crawl & Handcrafted Pasta

Savor organic stone-milled sourdough, olive oil and preserve tastings, local fruit specialties, and fresh pasta in downtown TC.

9:30 AM – 10:45 AM Artisanal Bakery

Common Good Bakery

Downtown Traverse City

Organic stone-milled sourdough pastries, whipped ricotta toast with blistered heirloom tomatoes, croque madame, and espresso drinks.

11:00 AM – 3:00 PM Gourmet Tasting Crawl

Downtown TC Gourmet Artisan Crawl

Front Street Covered Brick Storefronts

  • Fustini's Oils & Vinegars: Interactive tasting bar featuring EVOO and 18-year aged balsamics.
  • American Spoon: Small-batch Michigan fruit preserves, cherry butter, and condiments.
  • Cherry Republic: Cherry wines, hard ciders, cherry salsas, and dark chocolate cherries.
5:30 PM – 7:30 PM Italian Dinner Anchor

Flying Noodle

136 E Front St • Downtown TC

Cozy, vibrant Italian eatery in downtown Traverse City specializing in fresh, scratch-made pasta extruded daily.

🍝 Menu Highlights:
• Starters: Garlic Dots with butter and parmesan, Crispy Arancini, Whipped Ricotta.
• Fresh Pastas: Tagliatelle Bolognese, Rigatoni Alla Vodka, and Creamy Truffle Cacio e Pepe.

Friday Focus: Old Mission Peninsula Wine Trail & Waterfront Fine Dining

Five estate wine tasting stops along M-37 capped off with waterfront dining at Bowers Harbor.

9:30 AM – 11:00 AM Brunch

Red Spire Brunch House

The Village at Grand Traverse Commons

Stuffed French toast, savory omelets, and eggs Benedict inside historic Mercato.

11:30 AM – 1:00 PM Anchor Vineyard

Brys Estate Vineyard & Winery

3309 Blue Water Rd • Old Mission Peninsula

155-acre estate. Enjoy indoor seated tastings featuring Wine Tasting Towers, Reserve Gewürztraminer, Pinot Gris, and artisanal charcuterie.

1:15 PM – 2:15 PM Winery Stop

Bowers Harbor Vineyards

2896 Bowers Harbor Rd • Old Mission Peninsula

Unpretentious tasting room known for crisp Pinot Grigios, single-vineyard Rieslings, and estate hard ciders.

2:30 PM – 3:30 PM Winery Stop

Chateau Chantal

15900 Rue de Vin • Old Mission Peninsula

Perched on a high ridge offering panoramic bay views, sparkling wine flights, and Michigan ice wines.

3:45 PM – 4:45 PM Winery Stop

Bonobo Winery

12011 S Center Rd • Old Mission Peninsula

Rustic-chic lodge perched high with West Bay vistas. Features estate Pinot Gris, Sauvignon Blanc, Chardonnay, and small plates.

5:00 PM – 6:15 PM Cave Tour & Flight

Mari Vineyards

8175 Center Rd • Old Mission Peninsula

Italianate stone estate featuring underground wine barrel caves. Enjoy a cave tour and seated flight of estate wines.

6:30 PM – 8:30 PM Waterfront Fine Dining

The Boathouse Restaurant

2816 Bowers Harbor Rd • Bowers Harbor

Cottage-style elegance on Bowers Harbor. Highlights include pan-seared Great Lakes whitefish, Australian rack of lamb, oysters, and prime steaks.

Saturday Focus: Oaky's Tavern, Leelanau Wineries, Hop Lot & Aerie Steakhouse

Start with Oaky's smoked wings and brisket tacos, explore sparkling and estate wines, hit Hop Lot's outdoor fire pits, and finish at Aerie.

12:00 PM – 1:30 PM Sit-Down Tavern Lunch

Oaky's Tavern

9205 US-31 • Grawn

Local legend for house-smoked BBQ and tavern comfort food.

🍗 Must-Try Dishes:
• Jumbo Wings: Smoked daily, tossed in Dry Rub, Honey Jalapeño, or Chipotle BBQ.
• Smokehouse Mac: Cavatappi tossed in house cheese sauce, topped with smoked pulled pork.
• Tacos & Burgers: Brisket Tacos, Thai Pork Tacos, or half-pound Olive Burgers.
2:00 PM – 3:00 PM Sparkling Winery

MAWBY Sparkling Wines

4519 Elm Valley Rd • Suttons Bay

Michigan's premier dedicated producer of traditional-method sparkling wines paired with savory bites.

3:15 PM – 4:15 PM Estate Winery

Shady Lane Cellars

9580 E Shady Ln • Suttons Bay

100% estate-grown, SIP-certified sustainable Dry Riesling, Pinot Blanc, and estate white blends in a stone building.

4:30 PM – 5:45 PM Fireside Craft Beer

Hop Lot Brewing Co.

658 S West Bay Shore Dr • Suttons Bay

Soaring pine grove beer garden with outdoor campfire pits, craft IPAs, hot mulled cider, soft pretzels with beer cheese, and s'mores kits.

6:30 PM – 8:30 PM 16th-Floor Ultra-Upscale Steakhouse

Aerie Restaurant & Lounge

100 Grand Traverse Village Blvd • Acme Tower (16th Floor)

360-degree glass views of Grand Traverse Bay. Prime cuts including Bone-In Ribeye, Center-Cut Filet Mignon, Wagyu Strip, lobster tails, and craft cocktails.

Autumn Travel & Seasonal Guidelines

Early Sunsets (5:15 PM)

Plan winery transitions between 11:00 AM and 4:30 PM to maximize daylight.

Peninsula Driving

Allow 20–30 minutes between Old Mission and Leelanau Peninsula venues.

Layered Outerwear

Ideal for Hop Lot's outdoor campfire pits in mid-November.
